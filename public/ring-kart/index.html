    Building the Eifel

    Up or W: gas. Down or S: brake and reverse. Left and right or A and D: steer. Space or Shift: drift; hold it through a corner and let go for a mini-turbo, blue then orange. C changes the camera (behind, high, on the bonnet), R puts you back on the road, Esc pauses, M mutes.

    The lap

    The Touristenfahrten lap, clockwise from the gate to the gantry on the Döttinger Höhe: Hatzenbach, the Flugplatz, down the Fuchsröhre to Adenauer Forst, the drop to Breidscheid, the 300 m climb up Kesselchen, the banked Karussell, Hohe Acht, Brünnchen and the jumps at Pflanzgarten, then the long straight home.

    Traced by hand from public maps and elevation profiles, so the order of the corners, their direction and the big climbs and drops are right, and the exact lines are approximate. The whole lap is shrunk to a bit over a third of its real 20.8 km so it takes about three minutes, and the hills a little less, so they stay steep.

    How it drives

    Arcade, like a kart game. The car goes where you point it, and grip limits how fast it can turn: ask for more and the front washes wide. Drift to turn tighter than grip allows and charge a mini-turbo. Tuck in behind a car to catch its tow. Crests fast enough send you flying. The grass is slow and the Armco is unforgiving.

    Every car has its own top speed, acceleration, grip and braking, in the right order but not test figures: the GT3 RS is the fastest thing in the lot, and the Volvo 240 is a Volvo 240.

    The rules of the road

    As on the real Touristenfahrten: slower cars keep right and you pass on the left. The cars that went through the gate before you are your race; the ones behind you will come past if you dawdle. Position counts who is furthest round the lap, and the clock runs from your barrier to the gantry.
