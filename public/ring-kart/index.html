Ring Kart

The Nordschleife, Laguna Seca and the Coast Road

Pick a track and a car. On the Nordschleife, join the queue at the Touristenfahrten gate, tap your ticket, and race the cars that went in ahead of you round the Green Hell to the gantry, past every Trabant and Transit doing the lap at their own pace. At Laguna Seca, three laps from a grid with the Corkscrew in every one. On the Coast Road, a sprint from Surf Town to the lighthouse with the sea on your left.

    Building the track

    Up: gas. Left and right: steer. Space: brake (Down brakes too, and reverses). Shift: drift; hold it through a corner and let go for a mini-turbo, blue then orange. The card at the top calls the next corner, Left 1 a hairpin to Right 6 nearly flat, and shouts Brake when you are too quick for it. E honks the slow cars out of the way, Q looks back, G puts the gas on for you, C changes the camera (behind, high, on the bonnet), R puts you back on the road, Esc pauses, M mutes.

    The lap

    The Nordschleife's Touristenfahrten lap, clockwise from the gate past Hatzenbach, the Flugplatz, down the Fuchsröhre to Adenauer Forst, the drop to Breidscheid, the climb up Kesselchen, the banked Karussell, Hohe Acht, Brünnchen and the jumps at Pflanzgarten, then the whole Döttinger Höhe home. Laguna Seca: the Andretti Hairpin, the Rahal Straight, the plunge down the Corkscrew, Rainey Curve. Both are the real roads from the map with real heights, shrunk (the Ring to a third, Laguna to 60%) so a race takes minutes.

    The Coast Road is made up: switchbacks up the cliff at Seal Rock, three dune jumps, Palm Alley and Smugglers' Bend, and up Lighthouse Hill to the finish.

    How it drives

    Arcade, like a kart game. The car goes where you point it, and grip limits how fast it can turn: ask for more and the front washes wide. Drift to turn tighter than grip allows and charge a mini-turbo. Tuck in behind a car to catch its tow. Crests fast enough send you flying. The grass is slow and the Armco is unforgiving.

    Every car has its own top speed, acceleration, grip and braking, in the right order but not test figures: the GT3 RS is the fastest thing in the lot, and the Volvo 240 is a Volvo 240.

    The rules of the road

    As on the real Touristenfahrten: slower cars signal right, move over and let you pass on the left, mostly, and not in tight corners. On the Ring, the cars that went through the gate before you are your race and the clock runs from your barrier to the gantry. Elsewhere you start sixth on a grid of eight and go when the five red lights go out. Position counts who is furthest round.
