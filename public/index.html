Hockey Strategy Lab v0.71

Bruins vs Canadiens

A hockey simulation for testing game plans. Every player is a real 2025-26 Bruin or Canadien, rated from public data, and every decision on the ice comes from what the player can see.

Game plan lab

Set each team's plan: forecheck, breakout, entry, coverage in both zones, power play, penalty kill and aggression. Run 100 games as the baseline, change a plan, and test it through the same 100 games. The answer is plain: a clear edge, too close to call, or a clear cost. Find the best option ranks every choice for one part of the plan, and any of the games can be watched on the rink or reviewed shot by shot.

Watch a game

A full game on the rink with real lines, line changes, penalties, offsides and icings. Turn on sight lines to see what the puck carrier sees: his shot, his passes and what each one is worth. Sim to the final horn when you only want the result.

Review a game

Any game you watched or the lab played, every shot judged by the shooter's own read: was there a better pass? Replay the moment, then play out the pass he passed up 200 times to see whether it would really have been better.

How it is built

Shots are scored by an expected-goal model fit on two seasons of NHL shots. Ratings come from MoneyPuck, skating speeds from NHL EDGE, and each goalie's save rates from his real numbers by shot danger. A game depends only on its seed, so any result replays exactly, in any browser. The sim is calibrated at NHL settings and compared with the real teams in plain view, misses included: it still creates about 30% more chances than real defenses allow.

Cathedral Lab v0.8

Notre-Dame and Amiens, stone by stone

Build the whole of Notre-Dame or Amiens in 3D, piece by piece, the way the masons did, from the footings to the spire. Every stone carries real load: strike the centering and the line of thrust appears, color by load and the piers glow at the base. The physics is a rigid-block masonry solver, checked against the classical result for a semicircular arch.
