Simulations and experiments

Interactive models and side projects, mostly built with AI coding tools. Each one runs in your browser, no install, and the data and models behind it are open to inspection.

Bruins vs Canadiens v0.32

Strategy against strategy, by the record. Set the systems each bench runs, let the engine play the matchup 10 to 100 times, and read wins, losses and overtime losses with a 95% interval, goals and expected goals for and against, and a reality check of those games against the clubs' real 2025-26 rates and records. Sweep every option for one card to see which forecheck or breakout holds up against that opponent. Underneath is a 5-on-5 hockey engine you can watch: real rosters rated from MoneyPuck data, skating calibrated to NHL EDGE tracking, an expected-goal model fit on two seasons of shots, line changes, fatigue, officials and the rulebook.

Play Review v0.6

A real Bruins game, every shot scored by expected goals against the best pass the model saw from the same spot, with a replay of the seconds before and after each one. Built from the NHL play-by-play and shift chart, the sim's systems and the same expected-goal model. Positions are inferred and the movement is a reconstruction, and the page says so. First step toward counterfactual play review.
