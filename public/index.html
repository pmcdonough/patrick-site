Interactive models and side projects, mostly built with AI coding tools. Each one runs in your browser, no install, and the data behind it is open to inspection.

Bruins vs Canadiens v0.29

A 5-on-5 hockey engine built on 2025-26 data. Skaters rated from real stat lines, shots graded against MoneyPuck expected goals, goalies at their actual save percentages, skating calibrated to NHL EDGE tracking. Pick each team’s systems mid-game and grade the results against the real teams.

Play Review v0.1

A real Bruins game, every shot scored by expected goals against the best pass the model saw from the same spot. Built from the NHL play-by-play and shift chart, with an expected-goal model fit on two seasons of MoneyPuck shot data. First step toward counterfactual play review.
