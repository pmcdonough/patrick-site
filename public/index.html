Simulations and experiments

Interactive models and side projects, mostly built with AI coding tools. Each one runs in your browser, no install, and the data and models behind it are open to inspection.

Bruins vs Canadiens v0.42

Test hockey strategies before game day. Set each team's plan, from the forecheck and breakout to the penalty kill, then play two versions of it through the same 100 simulated games. You get a plain answer: a clear edge, too close to call, or a clear cost. Watch any game on the rink with real 2025-26 rosters, and turn on sight lines to see what the puck carrier sees. Every number is checked against what the real teams did.

Play Review v0.8

Every shot in a real game, asked one question: was there a better play? The model prices the shot that was taken, then the pass or the drive that was available from the same spot, and shows the gap in a replay you can scrub through in slow motion, the real play with the alternative ghosted over it. Built from the NHL's own play-by-play and the same expected-goal model as the sim. The positions are inferred and the page says so; what it points at is which clips are worth pulling.
