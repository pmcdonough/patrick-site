The red line is the line of thrust through the vault's ribs, down the wall, along each flyer and through each buttress pier, worked out the same way as in How an arch stands. They appear when the vault's centering is struck, the moment the vault starts carrying itself.

Color by load follows each piece's weight down to the ground and divides by the area it rests on. It ignores the sideways push, so it shows how load piles up, not exact stresses.
