What you're building

Three bays of the nave: round piers, the arcade, the gallery, the clerestory, a four-part rib vault in each bay, flying buttresses to outer piers topped with pinnacles, and the oak roof frame. Dimensions are close to the real nave (about a 12 m span and a vault crown near 35 m) but simplified: one aisle a side instead of two, and four-part vaults instead of the six-part ones Notre-Dame actually has.

The forces

The red lines are the line of thrust through the vault's ribs, down the wall, along each flyer and through each buttress pier, worked out the same way as in the arch lab. They appear when the vault's centering is struck, the moment the vault starts carrying itself.

Color by load follows each piece's weight down to the ground and divides by the area it rests on. It ignores the sideways push, so it shows how load piles up, not exact stresses.
